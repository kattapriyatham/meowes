Delete your Meowes account

The fastest way: in the app

  1. Open Meowes and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you will be signed out. You cannot sign back in.

If you can't access the app

Email meowesapp@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will verify ownership and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, name and phone number
  • Your virtual pet, its coins, items and activity history
  • Your friends list and group memberships
  • Settlements that no one has confirmed yet
  • Your sign-in — the account can no longer be used

What is kept, and why

Expenses and confirmed settlements you shared with other people are retained but anonymised (your name becomes “Deleted user”). Those are financial records the other person still relies on to know what they owe or are owed. They contain no personal identifiers of yours after deletion.

Backups

Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days.
