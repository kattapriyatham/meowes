Questions, Answered

When can I download Meowes?

We're finishing testing right now. Follow along and we'll share the moment it's live on iOS and Android.

Is Meowes free?

Yes. Adding friends, splitting expenses, and caring for your cat are all free.

Which platforms will it support?

iOS and Android at launch, with your account synced across both.

Is my expense data private?

Yes. Your balances and expenses are only visible to the friends and groups you choose to share them with.

Why a virtual cat?

Splitting bills is a chore. Settling up also feeds, pets, and levels up your cat, so there's a reason to open the app beyond owing someone money.
