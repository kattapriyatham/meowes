Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This policy explains what data the Meowes mobile app (“Meowes”, “we”, “us”) collects, why, and your choices. Meowes is operated by Priyatham Katta, an individual developer based in India. Contact: meowesapp@gmail.com.

We do not collect precise location, contacts from your address book, advertising identifiers, or biometric data. Meowes contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

How your data is used

  • To provide the app: sync your expenses, balances, friends, groups and pet across your devices.
  • To show shared expenses and settlements to the other people involved in them.
  • To send notifications you enable (a friend added an expense, your pet is hungry, reminders).
  • To keep the service secure and investigate abuse reports.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who your data is shared with

  • Other Meowes users you interact with — friends see your name, avatar and the expenses/settlements you share with them; group members see your name and the group's shared expenses.
  • Supabase (database & authentication hosting) — our data processor. supabase.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google / Apple — only for the sign-in you choose.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account (see below) your profile, pet, friends list, devices and unconfirmed settlements are deleted immediately. Expenses and confirmed settlements you shared with other people are retained but anonymised — your name is replaced with “Deleted user” — because the other party still needs those records for their own balance. Backups roll off within 30 days.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the personal data listed above; you cannot sign back in. If you can't access the app, use the account deletion request page or email meowesapp@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to certain processing. Email meowesapp@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Meowes is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to the database is restricted by row-level security so each user can only reach their own data and data explicitly shared with them.

Changes

We'll update this page and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
