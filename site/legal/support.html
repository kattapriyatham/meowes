Common questions

  • How do I delete my account? Profile → Delete account, or see the deletion page.
  • How do I report someone or something? Open the expense, group, or friend and use the flag / “Report” action. To block someone, open their profile → Block.
  • Does Meowes move money? No. “Settle up” only records a payment you made outside the app.
  • I'm not getting notifications. Check that notifications are enabled for Meowes in your phone's Settings.
