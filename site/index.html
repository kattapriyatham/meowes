What is Meowes?

Meowes helps you split expenses with friends, keep track of shared balances, and record settlements. You also get a virtual cat to care for along the way.

Is Meowes free to use?

Yes. Adding friends, splitting expenses, and caring for your cat are all free.

When can I get the app?

We’re finishing testing. Android is coming first, with iOS planned for a later release. If you’d like to know more, get in touch with our team.

Who can see my shared expenses?

Your shared balances and expenses are visible to the friends and groups involved. Read our privacy policy for more about how we handle your information.

What does the cat do?

Your cat gives you a little reason to check in beyond the bills. Pet them, feed them, and spend earned coins on treats and activities.
